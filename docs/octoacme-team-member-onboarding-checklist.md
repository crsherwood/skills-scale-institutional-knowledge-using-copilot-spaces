# OctoAcme — Team Member Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist helps new team members get oriented and productive quickly, ensuring they understand their role, have access to necessary resources, and know how to collaborate effectively with other roles.

## When to Use
Use this checklist when onboarding any new team member to an OctoAcme project.

---

## Pre-Start (Project Manager)
- [ ] Add team member to project communication channels (Slack, Teams, email lists)
- [ ] Grant access to project repositories, boards, and documentation
- [ ] Schedule first-day orientation and welcome meeting
- [ ] Assign onboarding buddy from the same or complementary role
- [ ] Share project one-pager, roadmap, and current sprint goals

---

## Week 1: Orientation and Setup

### For All Roles
- [ ] Complete welcome meeting with Project Manager
- [ ] Review project one-pager and understand success metrics
- [ ] Read all project documentation in docs/ folder
- [ ] Set up development environment (if applicable)
- [ ] Introduce yourself to the team in standup or team meeting
- [ ] Review [roles and personas documentation](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md)
- [ ] Understand communication cadence (standups, syncs, demos)
- [ ] Add yourself to team roster and contact list

### Role-Specific Tasks

#### Developers
- [ ] Clone repositories and verify local build works
- [ ] Review coding standards and PR workflow
- [ ] Set up CI/CD access and understand deployment process
- [ ] Pair with senior developer on first task
- [ ] Review Definition of Done for the project

#### Product Managers
- [ ] Review product vision and current roadmap
- [ ] Meet key stakeholders and understand their priorities
- [ ] Understand success metrics and how they're measured
- [ ] Review backlog and current prioritization
- [ ] Schedule regular syncs with PM lead and engineering lead

#### Project Managers
- [ ] Review project plan, timeline, and milestones
- [ ] Understand risk register and current blockers
- [ ] Review status reporting format and schedule
- [ ] Meet with stakeholders to understand expectations
- [ ] Familiarize yourself with project tracking tools

#### Business Analysts
- [ ] Review existing requirements documentation
- [ ] Understand business context and key workflows
- [ ] Meet Product Manager to align on requirements process
- [ ] Review traceability between requirements and user stories
- [ ] Identify upcoming requirements gathering sessions

#### UX Designers
- [ ] Review existing design system and component library
- [ ] Understand target users and personas
- [ ] Review accessibility requirements (WCAG standards)
- [ ] Meet Product Manager to understand product vision
- [ ] Review upcoming features needing design work

#### Scrum Masters
- [ ] Observe current sprint ceremonies
- [ ] Review team velocity and capacity planning approach
- [ ] Understand team's definition of done and working agreements
- [ ] Review retrospective action items and progress
- [ ] Meet with team to understand current impediments

#### DevOps Engineers
- [ ] Review infrastructure architecture and documentation
- [ ] Get access to CI/CD systems and cloud environments
- [ ] Review deployment procedures and rollback plans
- [ ] Understand monitoring and alerting setup
- [ ] Review incident response procedures

#### QA Leads
- [ ] Review test strategy and current test plans
- [ ] Understand test environments and how to access them
- [ ] Review defect tracking process and current open issues
- [ ] Meet with Developers to understand testability practices
- [ ] Review release readiness criteria

---

## Week 2: Active Contribution

### For All Roles
- [ ] Attend all regular ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews)
- [ ] Complete first meaningful contribution to the project
- [ ] Ask clarifying questions and document answers
- [ ] Provide feedback on onboarding experience
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 with manager to discuss progress

### Role-Specific Milestones

#### Developers
- [ ] Submit first pull request (can be small fix or documentation)
- [ ] Complete code review on a teammate's PR
- [ ] Understand how to run and write tests
- [ ] Participate in technical design discussion

#### Product Managers
- [ ] Present backlog item refinement to team
- [ ] Participate in sprint planning
- [ ] Meet with at least two key stakeholders
- [ ] Review and provide feedback on acceptance criteria

#### Project Managers
- [ ] Deliver first status update
- [ ] Update risk register with newly identified risks
- [ ] Facilitate or co-facilitate a team meeting
- [ ] Identify and document a dependency

#### Business Analysts
- [ ] Document requirements for one backlog item
- [ ] Conduct stakeholder interview or workshop
- [ ] Create or update a process flow diagram
- [ ] Collaborate with Developers on solution approach

#### UX Designers
- [ ] Complete design review for one feature
- [ ] Create wireframes or mockups for upcoming work
- [ ] Conduct usability review of existing feature
- [ ] Update or contribute to design system

#### Scrum Masters
- [ ] Facilitate at least one ceremony (standup or retro)
- [ ] Remove or escalate one team impediment
- [ ] Track and report on team velocity
- [ ] Coach team member on agile practice

#### DevOps Engineers
- [ ] Make improvement to CI/CD pipeline or documentation
- [ ] Support deployment to staging or production
- [ ] Respond to monitoring alert or incident
- [ ] Review and update infrastructure documentation

#### QA Leads
- [ ] Create or update test cases for one feature
- [ ] Execute manual or automated tests
- [ ] Report and track defects through resolution
- [ ] Participate in release readiness discussion

---

## End of Month 1: Full Integration

### For All Roles
- [ ] Confidently working on regular tasks without constant guidance
- [ ] Attending and contributing to all team ceremonies
- [ ] Building relationships across roles
- [ ] Providing valuable feedback and suggestions
- [ ] Complete 30-day check-in with manager

### Success Indicators
- Team members know your name and role
- You can independently complete tasks appropriate to your role
- You understand how your work fits into project goals
- You know who to ask for help on different topics
- You've contributed to at least one successful milestone or sprint

---

## Continuous Onboarding

Even after the first month, continue to:
- Seek feedback on your contributions
- Expand your knowledge of adjacent roles and their challenges
- Participate in knowledge sharing and documentation updates
- Look for process improvements based on your fresh perspective
- Build deeper relationships with cross-functional team members

---

## Feedback Loop

After completing onboarding:
- [ ] Provide feedback on this checklist to Project Manager
- [ ] Suggest improvements based on your experience
- [ ] Document any gaps or unclear areas you encountered
- [ ] Share what worked well to help future team members
