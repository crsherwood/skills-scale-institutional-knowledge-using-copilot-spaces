# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business stakeholders and technical teams. They elicit and document requirements, analyze workflows, and ensure solutions align with business objectives and constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit requirements from stakeholders through interviews, workshops, and observation
- Document business rules, process flows, and functional specifications
- Analyze and model current and future-state workflows
- Validate that technical solutions meet business needs
- Collaborate with Product Managers on prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Support Project Managers in risk identification and dependency analysis

### Goals
- Ensure requirements are clear, complete, and testable
- Minimize rework by catching gaps early
- Facilitate mutual understanding between business and technical teams

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Business requirements documents and user stories
- Traceability matrices linking requirements to acceptance criteria
- Regular syncs with Product Manager and Developers

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on requirements and priorities, provides business context
- **Developers**: Clarifies requirements, validates solution proposals, answers questions
- **Project Manager**: Supports risk analysis, helps identify dependencies and constraints
- **QA Lead**: Collaborates on defining acceptance criteria and test scenarios

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers create intuitive, accessible, and satisfying user experiences. They design user flows, wireframes, and prototypes that balance user needs with technical and business constraints.

### Responsibilities
- Research user needs, behaviors, and pain points
- Design user flows, wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Ensure usability, accessibility (WCAG compliance), and visual consistency
- Conduct usability testing and iterate based on feedback
- Create and maintain design systems and component libraries
- Advocate for user needs throughout the development process

### Goals
- Maximize user satisfaction and task completion rates
- Reduce friction and support in user journeys
- Ensure accessibility for all users

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and collaboration sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Annotated mockups and interactive prototypes
- Accessibility audit reports

### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Partners on product vision, validates designs against success metrics
- **Developers**: Collaborates during implementation, provides design specs and assets
- **QA Lead**: Reviews designs for testability, provides feedback on usability issues
- **Business Analyst**: Coordinates on user workflows and requirements

---

## Scrum Masters

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams in agile practices. They ensure the team can work effectively and continuously improve their processes.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate agile ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- Remove blockers and impediments to team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Shield the team from external distractions and scope creep
- Track and communicate team velocity and capacity
- Drive continuous improvement through retrospective action items

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Foster self-organization and empowerment
- Build a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment logs and escalation paths
- Retrospective action items and tracking
- Team health metrics and velocity reports

### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on dependencies, communicates impediments that need escalation
- **Developers**: Removes blockers, facilitates collaboration, coaches on best practices
- **Product Manager**: Ensures backlog is ready and refined, manages scope changes
- **DevOps Engineer**: Escalates infrastructure or tooling issues, coordinates on process improvements

---

## DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and deployment automation. They enable reliable, fast, and repeatable software delivery while ensuring operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure as code (IaC) and configuration management
- Implement monitoring, logging, and observability solutions
- Ensure security best practices in build and deployment processes
- Support developers with environment provisioning and troubleshooting
- Coordinate release deployments and rollback procedures

### Goals
- Reduce deployment cycle time and increase deployment frequency
- Maximize system reliability and uptime
- Automate repetitive tasks and reduce manual intervention

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure change logs and deployment schedules
- Incident reports and postmortems
- Platform documentation and runbooks
- CI/CD pipeline status and metrics

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Provides development environments, troubleshoots build and deployment issues
- **QA Lead**: Coordinates on test environment setup, supports automated testing infrastructure
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on release schedules, communicates deployment risks
- **Scrum Master**: Collaborates on process automation and tooling improvements

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads plan and coordinate all testing activities to ensure software quality and reliability. They define test strategies, manage testing resources, and ensure comprehensive coverage across manual and automated testing.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test strategies and plans aligned with project goals
- Define and validate acceptance criteria with Product Managers
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Manage test environments and test data
- Track defects and quality metrics
- Provide go/no-go recommendations for releases
- Mentor QA team members on testing best practices

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal defects
- Maximize test coverage and efficiency
- Catch issues early in the development cycle

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Defect reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Release readiness assessments and risk reports
- Testing status updates in sprint reviews

### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Collaborates on testability, reproduces and validates defect fixes
- **Product Manager**: Validates acceptance criteria, prioritizes defects and testing scope
- **Project Manager**: Reports quality risks, coordinates test schedule and dependencies
- **UX Designer**: Provides feedback on usability issues discovered during testing
- **DevOps Engineer**: Coordinates on test environment setup and deployment validation
